About Low Stock Alerts

This report automatically identifies materials and items that have fallen below their configured reorder levels. Items are classified as "Critical" when current quantity is 50% or more below the reorder level, and "Out of Stock" when quantity is zero or negative. Use the suggested reorder quantities to replenish inventory.
