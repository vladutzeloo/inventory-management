Transaction Types
Receipt

Inventory received from suppliers or production. Increases stock levels and creates FIFO batches.

Transfer

Movement of inventory between locations or bins. Decreases source location and increases destination.

Adjustment

Manual corrections to inventory levels due to cycle counts, corrections, or other adjustments.

Scrap

Removal of damaged, expired, or unusable inventory. Decreases stock levels using FIFO costing.

About Transaction History

This report provides a complete audit trail of all inventory movements in the system. Use the filters to narrow down transactions by type, date range, or specific criteria. Each transaction shows the date, type, location, material/item, quantity change, user who performed the action, and any reference numbers or notes. Positive quantities indicate increases in stock, while negative quantities indicate decreases.
